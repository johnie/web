This site is a small personal home on the web for writing, notes, and a few pages about my work and interests.

## Built With

- **TanStack Start** for the app and routing
- **React** and **TypeScript** for the UI
- **Tailwind CSS** for styling
- **MDX** and **content-collections** for writing and page content

## Hosting

The site is built as a static-friendly app and deployed on modern edge infrastructure.

## Content

Most of the content on this site is written in Markdown or MDX, which keeps publishing simple and close to the code.

## Design

The design aims to stay minimal, readable, and fast, with an emphasis on strong typography and straightforward navigation.

## Maintenance

This site evolves over time. Pages, tools, and workflows change as my work and interests change.
