### I Coded by Hand This Weekend

Did something weird this weekend: I coded by hand.

No agents, just me, my mouse, tab completion, and dictation, slowly shaping TypeScript types until they looked the way I wanted.

Felt like our version of touching grass: 🌱

It took forever. But there was something liberating about it. I got to structure things deliberately, get the types right, and remember what it actually feels like to code.

Not orchestrate. Not review. Not prompt. Just code.

I am not going full raw dog mode from now on. The leverage we get from AI is too good to give up. But it was a good reminder that the craft still feels nice when you sit with it for a while.
